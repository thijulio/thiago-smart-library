hands-on usability, security, performance, pricing or paid-plan validation.
Vendor descriptions establish advertised capabilities, not tested quality.
A feature missing from these pages is not proven absent from the product.

## Implications — our interpretation

1. **Keep the existing thesis.** Durable books, reactions and reviewable
   preferences give a coherent direction. Demand is not validated yet.
2. **Complete a useful reading loop.** Empty libraries need capture; returning
   readers need current reading, a next choice and retrieval of past feedback.
3. **Attach AI to records.** MCP is a channel, not a unique selling point.
   Identify the reactions/sources supporting a suggestion and allow disagreement.
4. **Preserve trust and portability.** Private ownership, distinct authorship
   and eventual export support our thesis. This is not a claim that competitors
   lack privacy.
5. **Limit breadth.** Social discovery, lending, an ebook reader and a general
   notebook each widen scope beyond the first reading-memory workflow.

## Hypotheses to validate

| Hypothesis                                                | Small useful observation                                                     | Consequence                                                      |
| --------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Readers want to retrieve their own book reactions         | Can a reader find a past reaction for a specific decision?                   | Improve detail/memory retrieval before semantic search           |
| Current reading and a queue add value beyond a catalog    | Can a reader select a next book from existing records?                       | Deliver Reading/Up Next                                          |
| Evidence-linked suggestions are useful and understandable | Compare one grounded suggestion with the reader's own choice and explanation | Evaluate one preference/enrichment slice; do not assume training |
| Assistant capture is worth connection effort              | Observe a consented capture task after browser capture works                 | Validate real MCP clients inside the product                     |

No recruitment messages, competitor accounts or paid subscriptions were
created. Numeric success thresholds and pricing need further evidence.
