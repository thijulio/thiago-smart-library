## Decisions and proposals remain separate

**Confirmed:** private multiuser ownership; Google first; founder-only populated
library today; record durability; grounded/distinguishable AI; nonblocking
enrichment; existing resources; Sheet authority until explicit M5 cutover.

**Recommended for review:** prioritize capture, reading, reflection and revisiting;
a reading workspace before broad discovery; a small invited pilot before AI expansion.

**Unresolved:** first external audience and pilot targets; whether native libraries
may become app-owned before the founder's cutover; any change to full-parity
requirements; first evaluated AI slice; pricing. No new provider, embedding model
or paid inference commitment follows from the benchmark.

## Assistant direction

access from ChatGPT and Claude, with a separate planned Playground. Sign in
with ChatGPT is not a Google app login or an MCP connection. This review retains
the latest decision without assuming client eligibility, inference or access
